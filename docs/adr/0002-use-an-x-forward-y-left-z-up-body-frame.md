# Use an x-forward, y-left, z-up body frame

Use a right-handed Body Frame at the Aft Datum with +x toward the nose, +y toward the rocket's left, and +z toward its top; measure azimuth by the right-hand rule around +x starting from +z. This matches the ROS and Gazebo axis orientation intended for future adapters while giving geometry, actuator deflections, and renderers one explicit sign convention.
